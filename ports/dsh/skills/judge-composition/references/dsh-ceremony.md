# DSH judge ceremony checklist

## Before calls

- The delegation gate justifies the cost.
- Every prompt companion is loaded without duplicate `skill` calls.
- The candidate is filed as verbatim addressed bytes.
- The driving question, claim modes, evidence allowlist, and closed drawback classes are fixed.
- The characterizer and composer are cold children and cannot recover the candidate's identity from workspace artifacts.
- Pre-registration is stored outside every judge-visible path.
- Each role's visibility matrix records prompt, files, skills, instructions, expected answer, authorship, and other verdicts.

## Call scheduling

Emit grounding, coherence, and corroboration calls together when their evidence is ready. Set `run_in_background: false` when their outputs are required to build the audit input. Do not fork the parent: completed history can contain the hidden candidate, expectations, or authorship.

## Audit input

Audit receives the exact seat prompts, admitted artifacts and digests, composition/anchor artifacts, masking and curation disclosures, pre-registration, raw verdicts, abstentions, and non-spawn rationales. It looks for rubric gaming, convention blindness, systematic drift, coverage holes, filing tilt, and leakage. Its output is advisory evidence, never a gate by itself.

## Refusal conditions

Stop with a disclosed limitation when cold spawn is absent, a hidden variable is still reachable, a seat has no abstention or failure path, candidate-blind composition cannot be established, no verdict survives jurisdiction, or audit inputs are incomplete.
